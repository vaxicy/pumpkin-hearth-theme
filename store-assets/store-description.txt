Pumpkin Hearth Theme brings a warm autumn desk to VS Code: cream paper, coffee ink, pumpkin orange and wood brown. Two coordinated variants ship in one extension - a light theme for daytime with a burnt-orange status bar, and a dark theme on espresso with a bright pumpkin band for the evening. Syntax colors come from the same palette: olive strings, rust keywords, wood-brown numbers and plum types, each checked against the background it sits on. Semantic highlighting, the full terminal ANSI palette, Git decorations, search and selection colors are all themed, so the editor, side bar, panels and terminal read as one piece.
